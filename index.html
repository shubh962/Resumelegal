About the App

What is Pro Resume Maker?

Pro Resume Maker is a free Android application built specifically for job seekers in India and around the world. It lets you create clean, professional resumes from scratch — right on your phone — and export them instantly as PDF files ready to send to recruiters.

Most resume builders either cost money, force you to create an account, or produce resumes that look great on screen but fail when processed by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) — the automated software used by most companies to filter job applications before a human ever sees them. We built this app to solve all three problems at once.

Everything happens on your device. No internet needed for editing, no cloud storage, no login. Your resume data is completely private and stays with you. We show a few ads to keep the app free — that's the whole business model, nothing hidden.

ATS Compatibility

Why Our Templates Pass ATS Filters

  • Single-font PDF output using Helvetica — universally readable by ATS
  • Skills as plain bullet points, never tables or pill badges
  • Standard section headings: EXPERIENCE, EDUCATION, SKILLS, PROJECTS
  • Contact info in the main page flow, not PDF headers/footers
  • No images, charts, or progress bars in content areas
  • Left-aligned body text for maximum parsing accuracy
  • LinkedIn and website links included in the contact row
  • Clean date formats that ATS systems recognize correctly

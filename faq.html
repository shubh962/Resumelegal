Templates & PDF

For IT, corporate, and MNC applications, we recommend:

  • Clean ATS Pro — best ATS compatibility, plain and professional
  • Classic Pro — traditional single-column, universally accepted
  • Executive — black & gold, good for senior roles

Avoid heavily designed templates (like Sidebar Dark or Crimson Edge) for ATS-heavy application portals — the two-column layout can confuse some parsers.

Yes — all templates are designed with ATS compatibility in mind. Specifically:

  • Single standard font (Helvetica) — universally parseable
  • No tables, charts, or progress bars in content areas
  • Standard section headings: EXPERIENCE, EDUCATION, SKILLS
  • Contact info in the main page body, not in PDF headers/footers
  • Left-aligned text throughout

Single-column templates (Classic Pro, Clean ATS Pro) have the highest ATS compatibility. Two-column templates work well for human readers but may be parsed less accurately by some older ATS systems.

Data & Privacy

All your resume data is stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences storage. None of your personal information — name, phone, experience, education — is uploaded to any server. We don't have a backend database with your data. If you uninstall the app, the data is deleted.

Currently, resume data does not transfer automatically when you change devices or reinstall the app. We strongly recommend downloading your resumes as PDFs and saving them to Google Drive or emailing them to yourself before switching devices. This is a known limitation we're working to address in future versions.

Go to the Profile tab → scroll down to the Data section → tap Clear All Data. This permanently deletes all resumes and preferences from your device. Alternatively, uninstalling the app removes all data as well. This action cannot be undone, so export your PDFs first.

No. We don't collect the resume content you enter. The only data collected is standard ad-related data by Google AdMob (device type, general location for ad targeting) — which is the same as any free app with ads. See our full Privacy Policy for details.

Notifications & Settings

If you have Reminders enabled in the Profile screen, the app sends one motivational notification per day at a random time between 9 AM and 8 PM. These are gentle prompts to keep your resume updated. You can disable them anytime by going to Profile → Reminders → toggle off.

Open the app → tap Profile tab → find the Reminders toggle → turn it off. You can also disable notifications system-wide from your phone's Settings → Apps → Pro Resume Maker → Notifications.

Troubleshooting

Try these steps in order:

  • Force close the app and reopen it
  • Restart your phone
  • Check if there's an app update available on the Play Store
  • Clear the app cache: Settings → Apps → Pro Resume Maker → Clear Cache
  • If the issue persists, email us with your device model and Android version

A few things to check:

  • Make sure the app has storage permission enabled (Settings → Apps → Pro Resume Maker → Permissions → Storage)
  • Ensure you have enough free storage space on your device
  • Try sharing instead of downloading — this often works even when direct download doesn't
  • If an ad didn't load, the download may not trigger — check your internet connection

Why We Built This

Finding a job is already hard enough. The last thing you should be worrying about is whether your resume looks professional or whether an automated system is going to reject it before a human ever sees it.

We built Pro Resume Maker because most resume apps either cost money, require you to create an account, or produce resumes that look great on screen but get mangled by ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) — the software that most companies use to filter applications before they reach a recruiter.

So we focused on getting the fundamentals right: clean templates that ATS systems can actually parse, a simple editing flow that doesn't waste your time, and PDF export that you can email straight to a recruiter. All free, all on your device, no account needed.

We're a small independent project, not a VC-backed startup with a "freemium" agenda. We run a few ads to cover our costs and keep the lights on. That's the whole deal.

Our Commitment to ATS Compatibility

  • All templates output clean, single-font PDFs using Helvetica — a standard font ATS systems handle correctly
  • Skills are rendered as plain bullet points, not pill badges, tables, or columns
  • Section headings use standard keywords: EXPERIENCE, EDUCATION, SKILLS, PROJECTS — the terms ATS systems scan for
  • Contact information appears in the main page flow, not in PDF headers or footers (which many ATS systems ignore)
  • No charts, progress bars, or graphical elements in the content area
  • Left-aligned body text for maximum parse accuracy
